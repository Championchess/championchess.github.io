Across these directions

My research spans foundation and generative models for physical understanding and embodied interaction. I work on vision-language reasoning, efficient grounding, and reinforcement learning, diffusion and video representations, and data curation. My experience also includes dataset and benchmark design, robotic manipulation, and navigation.
